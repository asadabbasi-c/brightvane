Approach

Spec first. Ship fast.
Then compound.

Every product starts as a written PRD. It keeps us honest about the problem before a single screen is drawn.

01

Define

Market, user and economics written down — scope, success metrics, and what we won't build.

02

Design

Flows tested against the spec, not taste — simple enough to learn in one session.

03

Build

AI-accelerated engineering on proven stacks, in production in weeks rather than quarters.

04

Grow

Launch is the midpoint. SEO, stores, outreach and iteration until the product stands on its own.
